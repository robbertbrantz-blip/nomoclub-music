FAQ

Frequently asked

How long does it take?

Standard delivery is within 5 days. Need it sooner? With rush you'll have it within 24 hours.

When do I pay?

You first hear a free sample of your song. If you want the full version, you pay via a secure payment link and receive the complete song right away.

How can I pay?

Securely via Stripe — iDEAL, Bancontact, credit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay, depending on your country.

Can I use my own photo for the cover?

Yes. With the For the World option you can give us your own photo, or we design a cover for you.

What if I'm not happy?

Thanks to the sample, you hear how your song sounds before you pay. If it's not for you, there's nothing to pay.

Do you make songs for a memorial?

Yes, with great care. Please reach out and we'll find the most fitting tribute together.

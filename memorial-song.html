When do you have a memorial song made?

A memorial song is often made for a funeral or celebration of life: an original song about that one person, instead of a song everyone already knows. But just as often it comes after — as a tribute song for an anniversary of their passing, behind a photo video, or simply to play at home when the missing feels heavy.

And sometimes the song is made while there is still time together. To listen to together, to say the things that otherwise stay unsaid, and to carry a gentle goodbye later.

How it works

  1. Tell us your story. The memories, the character, the moments you will never forget — in your own words, it does not have to be perfect.
  2. We write and create the song. With care, and a real person reads every story.
  3. You hear a free sample. So you know exactly how it sounds before you pay anything.
  4. Pay and receive the full song. Standard within 5 days — rush within 24 hours if the funeral is sooner.

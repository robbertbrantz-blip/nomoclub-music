Who do you have a birthday song made for?

For your partner, your mum, your best friend or your child. For a milestone birthday — someone turning 50, 18 or 80 — or simply for someone who deserves a little extra love this year. The more life there is to tell, the more beautiful the song.

A personalized birthday song isn't "Happy Birthday to You" — it's their story, sung. The holiday that became legendary, the sayings everyone knows, the things you're secretly so proud of. Joyful enough for the party, warm enough for a tear.

How it works

  1. Tell us their story. The memories, the anecdotes, what they mean to you — in your own words.
  2. We write and create the song. With care and feeling, and a real person reads every story.
  3. You hear a free sample. So you know exactly how it sounds before you pay anything.
  4. Pay and receive the full song. Standard within 5 days — rush within 24 hours if the birthday is sooner.

Frequently asked

Should a birthday song be funny or moving?

That's up to you — or both. A joyful singalong for the party, a warm song about how much they mean to you, or something with a smile and a tear. We match the tone to your story.

What about a milestone birthday?

Especially then! A 50th, an 18th, an 80th — the bigger the year, the more life there is to capture in a song. Tell us the stories and anecdotes, and we weave them into one.

Can the song be played at the party?

Yes. You receive the song as an audio file — perfect to play through the speakers, put behind a photo slideshow, or sing along to with everyone.

How quickly is the birthday song ready?

Standard within 5 days. Is the birthday sooner? With rush delivery your song is ready within 24 hours (+€50 / £45 / $55).

For the first dance, of course — an opening dance to a song that is actually about the two of you is something no guest forgets. A personalized wedding song is your story, sung. The night it began, the question that changed everything, the promise you make each other. Beautiful on the day itself — and forever after, behind your wedding video too.

Frequently asked

Can the wedding song be our first dance?

Yes — that might be the most beautiful moment for it. You receive the song as an audio file, and we match the tempo and mood to the dance you have in mind: from a slow ballad to a joyful opening dance.

Can I surprise my partner with it?

Yes. You tell us your story in secret — how you found each other, what you want to promise them — and on the big day a song plays that nobody saw coming.

Can we gift a wedding song as parents or friends?

Absolutely, it happens often. Tell us the couple's story and we turn it into a wedding song — a gift that beats every speech.

How quickly is the wedding song ready?

Standard within 5 days. Is the wedding sooner? With rush delivery your song is ready within 24 hours (+€50 / £45 / $55).

What about a wedding anniversary?

Yes — a silver, ruby or golden anniversary deserves its own song just as much. Tell us the story of all those years together.

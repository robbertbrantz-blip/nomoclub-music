Good to know

Questions about your personal song

How soon will I receive my song?

Your song is usually ready within five days. Is there a special date, or very little time? Then we can deliver the song within 24 hours as a rush order.

Can I supply my own text or poem?

Yes. If you have written a speech, a letter or a poem, we turn it into the lyrics — that is the Your Words option. A spoken text becomes shorter along the way: around eight hundred spoken words become roughly two hundred sung ones. You also receive the song without vocals, so you can sing it yourself.

When do I pay?

You’ll first receive a preview of your song, free of charge. Take your time to listen and decide whether the words, the music and the mood fit your story. If you’d then like to receive the complete song, you simply pay through a secure payment link.

How can I pay?

Through a secure payment link — you can pay with PayPal or Mastercard.

What if the song doesn’t feel right?

You’re not tied to anything. If the first preview doesn’t speak to you, you don’t pay anything. Do tell us what you had expected to be different — together we’ll see whether we can bring the song closer to your story.

Can I choose how the song sounds?

Yes. You can tell us which musical style, mood or voice you love. You’re welcome to name a favourite song or artist as inspiration. We’ll make sure the result is a personal song of its own.

Can I use my own photo for the cover?

Yes. With the Complete Edition you can supply a personal photo. If you don’t have a suitable one, we’ll design a cover that fits the story and the mood of the song.

What moments do people have a song made for?

For moments when ordinary words don’t seem enough. To say that you love someone, or that you’re proud of them. As a personal gift, for a wedding or a birth, in illness or at a farewell, or in memory of someone who is missed. A song for yourself is possible too.

Can a song be made for someone who is terminally ill?

Yes. Sometimes there is still so much someone wants to say, but the right words are hard to find. A song can give love, gratitude and shared memories a lasting form — to listen to together now, or to keep for later.

Can a song be about someone who has died?

Yes. You can have a custom memorial song made for a funeral or a memorial, for the anniversary of a loss, or simply because you miss someone. It tells not only of the goodbye, but above all of who someone was, what you shared together and what remains.

Does my story stay private?

Your story is treated with care and kept personal. If you choose the Personal Song, the song stays private. Only with the Complete Edition is it released on the streaming services, and only with your consent.

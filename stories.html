Examples

Real stories, real songs

Every song below started with someone like you: a memory, a loss, or a love that wanted to be sung. Listen to our examples — from memorial songs to love songs and joyful singalongs. We share the stories anonymously, with respect for everyone who trusted us with them.

Our title song: Straight From The Heart

When you miss someone

Memorial songs and songs of remembrance for a loved one who is no longer here.

I Never Forget You

For a woman who lost her husband. They were engaged when they were young, lost touch, and found each other again years later — followed by twenty years of marriage. A song about loyalty, strength and love that stays.

You Were My Tomorrow

An intense song of love and loss, for a woman who lost her soulmate. He was her best friend and her future — and still feels close.

Still in the Room

About someone who is physically gone but still present: in the room, in memories, in small everyday moments.

The Call That Never Came

A universal song about waiting for a call or a sign that never came. About missing someone, distance and words left unspoken.

Roses Fall

Quiet and symbolic: roses stand for love, for loss, and for everything that remains after someone is gone.

For parents, brothers and sisters

Family songs: for a father or mother, a brother or sister — a keepsake in music.

The Stories You Told

For a father in heaven. About the stories he used to tell, his protective nature and the love he gave his family.

The Lighthouse Still Shines

Given as a gift to someone who lost his father at a young age. The lighthouse stands for a father who keeps shining and showing the way.

Still Your Mama

About the bond between a mother and her child that never changes — however old you grow, whatever life brings.

Always There, Gary

For a brother and best friend who passed away. About growing up in a big family, laughing together and always being there for each other.

Always My Sister

For a sister who was kind, caring and always there. Not a song about illness, but about her beautiful soul and the warmth that remains.

For love

Custom love songs: for your partner, a wedding, or a love that spans the distance.

Across the Distance

About long-distance love and a connection that lasts, even when you can't be together. Feeling each other, missing each other, holding on.

You Do The Same

A personal song about love, recognition and reciprocity — about someone who does for you what you do for them.

With a smile and a tear

Not every personal song is a tearjerker: funny and joyful stories deserve a song too.

My Dad Got Robbed

A light-hearted song in Irish pub style about a dad who got scammed by fake helpdesk fraudsters. Funny and recognisable, with a human touch underneath.

Four Paws, One Heart

For everyone who knows how much love fits in a pet: four paws, one heart.

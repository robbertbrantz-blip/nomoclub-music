Examples

Real stories, real songs

Every song below started with someone like you: a memory, a loss, or a love that wanted to be sung. Listen to our examples — from memorial songs to love songs and joyful singalongs. We share the stories anonymously, with respect for everyone who trusted us with them.

The song that describes what we do — Straight From The Heart

When you miss someone

Memorial songs and songs of remembrance for a loved one who is no longer here. Want to have a custom memorial song made? Tell us your story.

I Never Forget You

They were young and engaged when life pulled them apart — years later they found each other again, and were married for twenty years, until he was gone. This song sings what she still feels every day: I never forget you.

You Were My Tomorrow

He was her soulmate, her best friend, her tomorrow. When she lost him, the love simply carried on — this song keeps him close.

Still in the Room

For everyone who knows the feeling: someone is gone, and yet still everywhere. In the chair by the window, in the smell of coffee, in the quiet of the room.

The Call That Never Came

Sometimes you wait for a call that will never come. This song is for everything you still wanted to say — and for everyone who knows that feeling.

Roses Fall

Written in loving memory of a dearly missed man. Roses fall, but what they leave behind remains: love that doesn't fade.

For parents, brothers and sisters

Family songs: for a father or mother, a brother or sister — a keepsake in music.

The Stories You Told

For a father in heaven. The stories he used to tell, his protective heart, the love he gave his family — in this song it all lives on.

The Lighthouse Still Shines

A gift of love, for someone who lost his father far too young. The lighthouse is his dad: still shining, still guiding, still there — year after year.

Still Your Mama

However old you grow, however far you go: to your mother you'll always be her child. A song about the bond that never changes.

Always There, Gary

For a brother who was also a best friend. Growing up together in a big family, mischief and school stories, always there for each other. A song full of love and longing.

Always My Sister

For a sister with a beautiful soul. She was always there when you needed her — and in this song, she still is.

For love

Custom love songs: for your partner, a wedding, or a love that spans the distance. Want to have a custom love song or wedding song made? Tell us your story.

Across the Distance

For two people with miles between them and not an inch between their hearts. Feeling each other, missing each other, holding on — across any distance.

You Do The Same

For love that flows both ways: someone who carries you just as you carry them. Simply because that's how it is.

With a smile and a tear

Not every personal song is a tearjerker: funny and joyful stories deserve a song too. Also lovely as a birthday song.

My Dad Got Robbed

About a dad who got a call from 'the helpdesk'... and got scammed. An Irish pub-style singalong to laugh about it together — with a serious wink.

Four Paws, One Heart

For the little friend who's always waiting at the door. Anyone who's ever loved a pet knows: four paws, one heart.

Who do you have a love song made for?

For the love of your life, of course. For an anniversary, for Valentine's Day, for the day you first met. For a long-distance love you want to hold close, or for someone who needs to hear just how much they are loved. And honestly? The best reason is no reason at all — just because it's true.

A personalized love song isn't an off-the-shelf romantic track — it's your story, sung. The campsite where it began, the look across the table, the cup of tea every morning. That's what makes it land: it's about the two of you.

How it works

  1. Tell us your story. How you found each other, why you love them, the little things only you two know — in your own words.
  2. We write and create the song. With care and feeling, and a real person reads every story.
  3. You hear a free sample. So you know exactly how it sounds before you pay anything.
  4. Pay and receive the full song. Standard within 5 days — rush within 24 hours if the moment is sooner.

Frequently asked

Can I give the love song as a surprise?

Yes — that is exactly what it is made for. You tell us your story in secret, we create the song, and you choose the moment: a birthday, your anniversary, Valentine's Day or an ordinary Tuesday.

I'm not great with words. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Just tell us in your own words why you love them — the little things are often the most beautiful. We turn them into the words and the music.

Can the song sound like our kind of music?

Yes. Feel free to mention your favourite artist, your song, or the style that fits you both — from a calm ballad to a joyful singalong. We fine-tune everything together.

How quickly is the love song ready?

Standard within 5 days. Is the moment sooner? With rush delivery your song is ready within 24 hours (+€50 / £45 / $55).
